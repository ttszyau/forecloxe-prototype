How Forecloxe Tokenizes & Trades
Foreclosed Hong Kong Properties

A complete lifecycle — bank-controlled, investor-friendly, SFC-compliant.
No title transfer. Pure economic rights via smart contract.

1

Bank Valuation & Listing

Independent valuation → Bank sets reserve, token count (e.g. 6,000 × HK$1,000), public % (80%), auction timeline (12–24 months) & re-auction rules.

2

Token Issuance & Initial Offering

Compliant security tokens issued (ERC-3643 style) → Fixed-price sale to KYC’d investors → Master Participation Agreement signed → Bank retains portion for upside.

3

Secondary Trading Period

24/7 order-book market on Forecloxe → Limit/market orders → Price floats on sentiment & news → 0.5–1% commission → Continues until auction date.

4

Auction Execution

Trading locks → Platform runs final public auction for physical property → Bank sells asset traditionally or via platform.

5

Smart Contract Settlement

Net proceeds to escrow → Smart contract auto-distributes pro-rata to all token holders → Bank gets retained share + ownership proceeds.

6

Re-auction or Cancel

If no sale → Tokens unlock → Cycle repeats per bank rules (e.g. every 6 months) until sold or cancelled.
